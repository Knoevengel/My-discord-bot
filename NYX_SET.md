# Nyx Set Update

Nyx is implemented as a Shade 6★ HP-risk / lifesteal character.

## Passive — Blood Debt
- Blood Debt is a self resource, max 5 stacks.
- Each stack grants Nyx +5% damage.
- While Nyx is below 60% HP, she has 20% damage reduction.
- When Nyx ends a turn below 60% HP with at least 3 Blood Debt, 3 stacks are consumed and Night Of Blood Moon is automatically unleashed.
- The automatic ultimate uses Nyx's Blood Debt damage bonus from immediately before the 3-stack consumption, then leaves any remaining stacks intact.

## Skill 1 — Scythe Swing
- 90% ATK Shade damage to 1 enemy.
- Nyx sacrifices 5% Max HP (never below 1 HP).
- Gains 1 Blood Debt.

## Skill 2 — Blood Rain
- 110% ATK Shade damage to all enemies.
- Nyx restores 25% of the total damage dealt as HP.

## Skill 3 — Night Of The Blood Moon
- 250% ATK Shade damage to all enemies.
- It is configured as auto-only and is triggered by Blood Debt at end of turn.

No manual Skill 3 activation is used for Nyx; her passive automatically unleashes it when its condition is met.
