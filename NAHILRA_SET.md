# Nahilra Set

## Gaze of Stone
- 70% ATK Shade damage to one enemy.
- 30% chance to Petrify for 1 turn.
- A successful Petrify counts toward Nahilra's next Ultimate requirement.

## Grasp of Ruin
- 80% ATK Shade damage to one enemy.
- Slow for 2 turns.
- Slow reduces effective Speed by 20% while active.

## Monument of Silence
- 120% ATK Shade damage to all enemies.
- Can only be selected after Nahilra has successfully inflicted Petrify since her previous Ultimate.
- After damage, guarantees Petrify on one enemy for 1 turn.
- The guaranteed Petrify occurs after the Ultimate and therefore begins the requirement window for the next Ultimate.

## Petrify
- Target loses its action while Petrified.
- Purify/Cleanse removes Petrify.
